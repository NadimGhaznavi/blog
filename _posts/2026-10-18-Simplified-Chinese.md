I recently built [MyCount](https://mycount.osoyalce.com) to track website visitors to my projects. I found that that over 40% of my visitors were from China, and that 35% of those used a Chinese web browser localization setting.

![Pie chart showing Chinese Visitors](/images/2026-10-18-chinese-visitors.png)

So, I enhanced my [fork of the Minimal-Mistakes](https://github.com/NadimGhaznavi/minimal-mistakes) and added multi-language support. Then, with the help of ChatGPT, I translated my blog, this site, into Simplified Chinese!

To all of you reading this in Simplified Chinese: Welcome!!! I appreciate your interest! :)
