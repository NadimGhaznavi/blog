I have a future project in mind which will require a *Dynamic DNS* service. I created [BMDynIP](https://bmdynip.osoyalce.com) for that purpose. After building the service, I wanted a simple application to test it with. So I thought, **Website Visitor Analytics**!!! I've been very dissatisfied with what I can find from 3rd party sources. So I created [MyCount](https://mycount.osoyalce.com).

The site includes a [gallery](https://mycount.osoyalce.com/pages/gallery/) that shows off the nice visualizations.
